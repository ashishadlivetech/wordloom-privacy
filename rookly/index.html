Rookly Privacy Policy

Rookly: Offline Chess · Last updated 11 November 2026

Rookly: Offline Chess ("the app") is published on Google Play by YT Apps Team (Adlivetech).

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, upload or share any personal information.

  • There is no login and no account.
  • Your games, puzzle progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions, and it does not use the internet.
  • This version contains no advertising and no analytics.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all data the app has saved.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, analytics or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
